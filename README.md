# Build a Premium Optometrist Website — Ashni Optometrists

You are a senior digital creative director, brand designer, UX architect and React frontend developer.

Build a beautiful, premium, highly polished, mobile-first website for **Ashni Optometrists**, an optometry practice located at **51 Titren Road, Rossburgh, Durban, 4094, South Africa**.

This is a client presentation prototype intended to win approval for a full website, digital presence and e-commerce implementation. The first draft must feel like a real, beautifully executed optometry brand website, not a generic healthcare template.

## 1. Brand and creative direction

Business name: Ashni Optometrists

Positioning: Professional, accessible eye care with a tasteful, fashion-conscious eyewear retail experience.

Desired personality:
- Trustworthy and professional
- Warm and welcoming
- Modern and refined
- Simple and uncluttered
- Premium without feeling expensive or intimidating
- Appropriate for a community-focused South African optometry practice

Brand palette:
- Primary Royal Plum: #512B72
- Secondary Lavender: #B49BCB
- Light background: #F8F5FA
- Charcoal: #27212E
- White: #FFFFFF

Use purple in the logo, buttons, interactive accents and restrained decorative elements. Keep the overall website predominantly white and soft ivory with beautiful contrasts.

Typography: Modern, highly legible sans-serif for body text, paired with tasteful editorial typography for major headings.

Create a simple, sophisticated logo direction combining the wordmark "Ashni Optometrists" with a minimal glasses or lens-inspired symbol. Avoid obvious stock medical icons. Develop the logo as a reusable SVG design component.

Suggested tagline: "See Clearly. Look Beautiful. Live Confidently."

This is a proposed tagline for client review.

## 2. Design quality standard

Reference the visual quality of premium boutique eyewear brands and contemporary optical practices.

The design must have:
- Generous whitespace
- Excellent typographic hierarchy
- Large, beautiful lifestyle and eyewear imagery
- Clean product grids
- Premium but subtle motion effects
- Rounded yet sophisticated buttons
- Responsive layouts on mobile, tablet and desktop
- A clean, sticky navigation bar
- Elegant section transitions
- No unnecessarily complex dashboards or animations

Use appropriate high-quality eyewear imagery. Clearly distinguish sample stock photography from actual practice photos.

Prefer photography that reflects diverse South African customers.

Do not invent photos of the optometrist, practice or staff.

## 3. Website structure

Create the following working routes:

1. / — Homepage
2. /eye-care — Eye Care Services
3. /shop — Eyewear Collection
4. /lenses — Tinted and Anti-Glare Lens Options
5. /about — About the Practice
6. /contact — Contact and Directions

All pages must share a unified header, footer, typography and design system.

## 5. WhatsApp integration

WhatsApp is the most important conversion channel.

Create a persistent but unobtrusive floating WhatsApp button on mobile and desktop.

Create separate prefilled enquiry messages for:
- Eye test appointments
- Spectacle frame enquiries
- Tinted lens enquiries
- Anti-glare lens enquiries
- General questions

Use a single central configuration variable for the official WhatsApp Business number.

The number has not yet been provided. Do not invent a number or create links to an unrelated account.

While the number is missing, show a graceful demo interaction instead of a broken link. Once configured, use standard wa.me links with properly URL-encoded message text.

Do not request sensitive medical history or prescription details through the demo forms.

## 6. Shop experience

Build an interactive, visually polished shop preview.

Include:
- Product listing page
- Product detail pages or modal previews
- Filtering by eyewear category
- Product images
- Frame colour and style placeholders
- WhatsApp product enquiry
- Visible explanation of how fittings and orders are confirmed

Use clean reusable mock product data, structured so that a Shopify integration can replace the dataset later.

For the first demo, do not connect real payment gateways, store API credentials or activate checkout.

The final implementation should be designed for a Lovable + Shopify integration with compatible South African payment processing.

IMPORTANT: South African professional regulations govern online prescription spectacle sales, fittings and dispensing. For prescription eyewear, the interface should be based on frame browsing, enquiries, prescription verification and appropriate in-practice fulfilment rather than unrestricted direct checkout.

Direct online checkout should be reserved for items explicitly approved as eligible for sale.

## 8. Mobile UX and accessibility

The majority of users may visit from phones.

Prioritise:
- Fast loading
- Comfortable tap targets
- Accessible contrast and readable text
- Sticky or easy-to-reach appointment CTA
- Clear mobile navigation
- Clean cards and layouts
- Image optimisation
- Minimal layout shift
- Accessible alt text
- Keyboard-friendly interactive controls

The website should feel excellent on a Samsung Galaxy A-series phone, iPhone and desktop.

## 10. Healthcare content rules

Use ethical, factual healthcare copy.

Do not create fabricated testimonials or reviews.

Do not claim that Ashni is the best, cheapest or most advanced optometrist.

Do not invent medical scheme acceptance, qualifications, specific testing equipment, promotional prices or offers.

Do not add health guarantees or misleading lens protection claims.

Any actual promotions and healthcare-specific advertising must be reviewed for South African professional compliance before public launch.

## 11. Final presentation standard

I need this to look like a near-finished professional website when presenting it to a prospective client.

The homepage must deliver an immediate premium impression.

Make sure:
- All navigation works
- All pages render correctly
- Sample eyewear cards are polished
- The logo looks professional
- The purple palette feels premium rather than overwhelming
- The contact and directions flow is obvious
- The site is not littered with placeholder text
- Unverified facts remain clearly labelled or are omitted
- Mobile and desktop layouts are equally impressive

After completing the initial build, summarise:
1. What is implemented and working.
2. Which parts are demonstration-only.
3. What information is required from the client.
4. What needs to be connected after project approval.

Prioritise visual excellence, clarity, client confidence and conversion to WhatsApp enquiries.
